📚 Learn about your body

🌙 Your cycle in four phases

🩸 Menstrual

The uterine lining sheds. Energy can dip, so rest, warmth and iron-rich food feel good.

🌱 Follicular

Hormones rise and many people feel more energetic.

✨ Ovulation

An egg is released, usually 10–16 days before the next period. Timing varies.

🌙 Luteal

PMS symptoms such as bloating, mood changes or tiredness can appear.

💡 Myths & facts

🩺 When to see a doctor

Please see a doctor or gynaecologist for pain that stops daily life, very heavy bleeding or large clots, no period by 15, three or more missed periods, cycles under 21 or over 45 days, bleeding between periods or after sex, or foul discharge with itching or fever.

Go urgently for fainting, soaking a pad or tampon every hour for 2+ hours, or severe pain with fever. In India, Tele-MANAS is 14416 (free, 24/7).
